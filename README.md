# lily_design_web
My website will be a professional portfolio and business website for my wife, who specializes in graphic design and apparel design. The purpose of the site is to showcase her creativity, skills, and completed projects while making it easier for potential clients to discover her work and reach out about business opportunities.  The website will feature two separate portfolios, one dedicated to graphic design and the other to apparel construction, along with an About Me page that gives visitors a fun and personal look at who she is. A Contact page will include her social media accounts, email, business phone number, and eventually a project questionnaire to help potential clients describe their ideas and project requirements.  What makes this website unique is that it combines two creative disciplines into one organized, personalized experience while helping streamline the process of connecting with new clients.
